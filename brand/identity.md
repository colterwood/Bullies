# Blocky & Co. — Brand Identity

## Brand Name
**Blocky & Co.**

## Tagline
*"Built for blocky heads. Made for big hearts."*

## Secondary Taglines
- "Your bully's favorite store."
- "Because nobody loves harder than a bully."
- "The store that actually gets your dog."

## Brand Story
Bull breed owners know what it's like. The stares at the dog park. The landlord
clauses. The strangers who cross the street. But they also know what nobody else
does — that their blocky-headed, wiggle-butted, face-licking chaos machine is
the most loving creature on earth.

Blocky & Co. is built for that community. Every product we carry is chosen
because it works for these specific dogs: the pullers, the chewers, the
overheaters, the velcro dogs who follow you to the bathroom. We're not a
generic pet store. We're a bully store.

## Brand Voice
- Warm, funny, zero corporate energy
- Talks to the owner like a fellow bully person, not a customer
- Celebrates the breed — the blocky heads, the zoomies, the stolen socks
- Educational without being preachy
- Never stigmatizes; always celebrates

## Tone by Context
| Context | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Homepage hero | Proud, punchy, direct |
| Product pages | Helpful, specific to bully traits |
| Email | Conversational, playful |
| Social | Funny, relatable, community-first |
| Error/404 pages | Self-aware humor ("Your dog probably ate this page") |

## Color Palette
| Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Brindle Amber | `#C8722A` | Primary CTA buttons, accents |
| Charcoal Block | `#1E2329` | Headlines, navbar |
| Cream Coat | `#FAF4EC` | Backgrounds, light sections |
| Warm Stone | `#7A6A5A` | Body text, secondary text |
| Soft Fawn | `#E8D4B8` | Section dividers, borders |
| Success Green | `#3D7A5C` | Confirmation, badges |

## Typography
- **Headlines**: Playfair Display (serif) — authoritative yet warm
- **Body**: Inter (sans-serif) — clean, readable on mobile
- **Accent/Tags**: Space Grotesk — modern, punchy

## Logo Concept
Text-based logotype: "Blocky & Co." in Playfair Display
- The "B" can feature a subtle blocky head silhouette inside the letterform
- Optional icon: simplified bulldog silhouette with a heart in the chest

## Imagery Direction
- Action shots of bully breeds being goofy, not posed
- Owners and their dogs — diverse, real-looking
- Close-ups of the blocky face (this is aspirational content for the community)
- Never: aggressive poses, chain collars, fighting imagery
- Always: wiggles, zoomies, belly rubs, sleeping in funny positions
