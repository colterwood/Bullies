## Post Template 2: Educational Tip
*Format: Carousel (Instagram) or talking-head video (TikTok)*

### Caption — "Why Your Bully Pulls So Hard"

Your bully isn't bad on leash. Here's what's actually happening:

Bull breeds were selectively bred for strength and drive. That forward energy
isn't aggression — it's enthusiasm. The problem isn't the dog, it's the
equipment.

A regular collar transfers all that pull pressure to the throat. Bad for any
dog, especially brachycephalic breeds like Bulldogs and Frenchies.

What actually works:
→ Front-clip harness (redirects pull energy sideways, not into pain)
→ Consistent, short training sessions
→ Rewarding *loose leash moments*, not just correcting pulling

We designed the Blockhead Harness specifically for the deep chest and strong
pull of bully breeds. It's in our bio link.

## Post Template 4: Summer Safety (Seasonal — High Engagement)
*Format: Educational short video or static post*

### Caption

PSA for bully owners going into summer 🌡️

Your brachycephalic dog (English Bulldog, Frenchie, flat-faced Pittie) can
overheat WAY faster than other dogs.

Signs to watch for:
❌ Excessive panting (harder for them to cool through panting)
❌ Bright red gums
❌ Stumbling or confusion
❌ Excessive drool

What to do NOW before it gets hot:
✅ Never leave them in a car. Not even with windows cracked.
✅ Short walks in the morning or evening only
✅ Always have fresh water available
✅ Consider a cooling mat for inside

We stock the Chill Pad for exactly this reason. Pressure-activated, no water,
no freezing needed. Just cool.

Stay safe out there, bully fam. 🐾
